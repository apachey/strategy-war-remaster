# The original game

- Strategy War, real-time strategy for J2ME phones, 2004. Code: Jiří Mikula. Graphics: Oskovran, Tomas Krcha.
  Sound: Jiří Mikula. Published by redboss developers.
- 10 unique builds were analysed (v1.3, 1.3.2, 1.3.5 MIDP-1/2, Motorola port, a Chinese mod, Redboss v1.6.3).
  The full comparison, extracted sprites and mission renders are in the project files:
  `/mnt/project-files/strategy-war/analysis/versions.md` and `/mnt/project-files/strategy-war/original/`.
- Hidden cheats shown after finishing the campaign: `1298*` reveal map, `1298#` next level, `12986` +1000 money,
  `12989` repair the selected object.
